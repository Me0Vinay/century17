📊 Excel to JSON Converter

Convert your product catalog to JSON format for Century17Toys website

Recognised columns:

  • product_id - Unique product identifier
  • sub_product_id - Variant identifier (same as product_id if no variants)
  • product_name - Product name
  • image_link - Main product image (URL or Google Drive link)
  • image_front, image_top, image_side, image_projection - (Optional) extra views; blank ones fall back to image_link
  • youtube_video - (Optional) embedded video URL
  • size, fabric_type, color - Variant details
  • category_type - Product category
  • price - Price (numbers only, no currency symbol)
  • increment_by - (Optional) quantity added per click / MOQ (defaults to 1)
